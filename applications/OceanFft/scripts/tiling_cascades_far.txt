# Overdriven Gale, one far-field oblique view, then the same view with the finest cascades switched off one by one.
# Shows which cascade's foam forms the repeating far-field pattern. Run with --capture=DIR.
preset Gale
sea HeightScale 1.6
choppiness 1.5
sea WhitecapThreshold 0.8
sea FoamAmount 1.3
ui off
dt 0.0166667
time 30
pause on
wait 30
camera 30 20 400
wait 10
capture far_all
cascade 4 off
wait 5
capture far_without_4
cascade 3 off
wait 5
capture far_without_3_4
cascade 4 on
wait 5
capture far_without_3
quit
